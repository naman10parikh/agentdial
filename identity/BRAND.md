# agentdial — BRAND

## Name Rationale

**agentdial** — "dial" evokes: dialing a phone (giving agents a phone presence), dialing in (tuning/configuring), and the control-panel metaphor (dialing up the right channel). One word, lowercase, developer-tool register. Same naming register as `kubectl`, `vercel`, `ngrok`.

## Tagline

> **Dial your AI agent into every platform. One identity. Every channel.**

Short form: `One identity. Every channel.`

## Symbolic Connection

agentdial is the **PBX** (Private Branch Exchange) of the agent world — the switchboard that routes calls/messages between the outside world and the agent's intelligence. Just as a PBX abstracts carrier complexity from employees, agentdial abstracts platform complexity from agents.
